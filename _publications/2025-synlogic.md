**Junteng Liu**, Yuanxiang Fan, Zhuo Jiang, Han Ding, Yongyi Hu, Chi Zhang, Yiqi Shi, Shitong Weng, Aili Chen, Shiqi Chen, Yunan Huang, Mozhi Zhang, Pengyu Zhao, Junjie Yan, Junxian He.

*SynLogic: Synthesizing Verifiable Reasoning Data at Scale for Learning Logical Reasoning and Beyond.* arXiv, 2025.

We introduce a scalable approach for synthesizing verifiable reasoning data that teaches models to perform logical reasoning and transfers this capability to broader reasoning settings.
