*C-Eval: A Multi-Level Multi-Discipline Chinese Evaluation Suite for Foundation Models.* NeurIPS 2023.

C-Eval provides a broad, multi-level, multi-discipline benchmark for evaluating foundation models in Chinese across diverse subjects and difficulty levels.
