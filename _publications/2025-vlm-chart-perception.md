**Junteng Liu**, Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, Junxian He.

*On the Perception Bottleneck of VLMs for Chart Understanding.* arXiv, 2025.

We investigate the perception bottleneck of vision-language models that causes failures in chart understanding, providing insights for improving VLM visual reasoning. Code: [Vision4Chart](https://github.com/Vicent0205/Vision4Chart).
