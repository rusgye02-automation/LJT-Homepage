I am a **first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group**, advised by **Prof. Junxian He**. I graduated from **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)** in **June 2024**, where I was also previously advised by Prof. Junxian He during my undergraduate studies.

My research focuses on **natural language processing and machine learning**, with a particular interest in building systems and analyses that make large language models more reliable, truthful, and capable of reasoning.
